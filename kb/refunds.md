# Refund policy

## Refund windows
- Electronics: refunds within 10 days of delivery.
- All other categories: refunds within 30 days of delivery.

## Eligibility
Orders must be delivered before they can be refunded. Orders still in transit can be cancelled instead.
Each order can be refunded once. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Approvals
Refunds above 5000 INR require approval from a support specialist before they are issued.
